Features

Temporary contacts

  • Set a time limit when you save a contact
  • A time-left badge on every timed contact
  • A Timed tab for everyone on the clock
  • Make timed, extend or keep several contacts at once

Asks before it acts

  • A notification sheet lists contacts whose time is up
  • Hiding removes the contact from your phone's address book and keeps the full record in the app
  • Restore in one tap

Relationship memory

  • A timeline of notes and attachments on every contact
  • Typed notes: five built in, add your own, eight colours
  • Pin up to three notes per contact
  • Attach photos and files

Find anything

  • Search names, numbers, note text and note types
  • A Timeline tab with every note from every contact
  • Search the app's own settings too

Smart Capture

  • Share a place from Google Maps to start a contact
  • Tap Get business info to fill in phone and opening hours
  • Text recognition and entity extraction run on your device

Backup and recovery

  • A plain-text vCard safety copy saved on your device, optional
  • An encrypted backup you protect with a passphrase
  • A Recovery Center with a history of changes and one-tap restore
  • Merge duplicates and clean-up suggestions

Travel and languages

  • Phone numbers understand your home country and the country you are in
  • Travel mode can tag notes with where you were
  • Arabic and right-to-left layouts

Two ways to use it

  • Pick Full CRM or Safe Ledger when you first open the app
