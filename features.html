Complete Product Tour

Every feature built for cleaner contacts & deeper recall

Explore how Keep Awhile combines temporary contact timers, structured relationship notes, on-device Smart Capture, and multi-layered backups.

Temporary Contacts Expiry Controls Relationship Memory Global Search Smart Capture Backup & Recovery Travel & RTL Two Modes
01 · Timed Lifecycle

Temporary contacts

Keep short-term contacts reachable while you need them, without letting them clutter your permanent address book forever.

  • Set a time limit right when you save or edit a contact
  • Time-left badge on every timed contact showing days remaining
  • Dedicated Timed tab for everyone currently on the clock
  • Bulk actions to make timed, extend, or keep several contacts at once
02 · Your Rules on Expiry

Ask first by default, or auto-hide

Choose whether you want to review expired contacts yourself or let Keep Awhile tidy them up automatically—either way, nothing is ever permanently deleted behind your back.

  • Ask-first default: a notification sheet lists contacts whose time is up so you can choose Keep, Extend, or Hide
  • Optional auto-hide in Settings: prefer hands-free cleanup? Turn on auto-hide so expired contacts move out of your phonebook automatically
  • Non-destructive hiding removes the contact from your phone's system address book while keeping the full record and notes inside Keep Awhile
  • One-tap restore puts a hidden contact right back into your phonebook whenever you need them again
03 · Contextual Notes

Relationship memory

Turn every contact card into a structured log of quotes, promises, borrowed items, and warranty records.

  • Chronological timeline of notes and attachments on every contact
  • Typed notes: five built-in categories (Appointment, Call note, Payment, Quote, Warranty), plus custom types across eight colours
  • Pinned notes: pin up to three critical notes per contact so key details stay at the top
  • Rich attachments: attach photos (like a boiler serial plate or receipt) and files directly to a note
04 · Deep Search

Find anything

Even if you forget a contractor's name, you can find them by what they fixed or what tool you lent them.

  • Full-text search across contact names, phone numbers, note text, and note types
  • Global Timeline tab displaying every note from every contact, newest first
  • Settings search so you can jump straight to backup, mirror, or label options
05 · Fast Intake

Smart Capture

Start a contact from a map share or a business card without typing phone numbers and opening hours by hand.

  • Share from Google Maps directly into Keep Awhile to start a new contact
  • Get business info enriches phone numbers and opening hours from open map data with explicit candidate confirmation
  • On-device OCR: text recognition and entity extraction run locally on your phone

06 · Safety Net

Backup and recovery

Your local database is paired with multi-layered backups and version snapshots so you never lose a number or note.

  • Plain-text vCard safety copy (`shown.vcf` and `hidden.vcf`) saved on your device, optional and readable even if the app is uninstalled
  • Encrypted backup protected with your own passphrase
  • Recovery Center with a history of edits, snapshots, and one-tap restore for hidden contacts
  • Merge duplicates and smart clean-up suggestions
07 · Global Ready

Travel and languages

Designed for people who move between countries and languages without breaking local dialing rules.

  • Country-aware numbers understand both your home country and the country you are currently visiting
  • Travel mode can automatically tag timeline notes with the region where you met or added a contact
  • Arabic and right-to-left (RTL) native interface layouts

08 · Flexible Onboarding

Two ways to use it

Choose the workflow that fits how you manage people when you first open Keep Awhile.

  • Full CRM or Safe Ledger onboarding choice right when you start
  • Full CRM mode unlocks relationship timelines, typed notes, attachments, and timed contact management
  • Safe Ledger mode keeps a focused, protected contact ledger on your device
Mode A

Full CRM View

Complete relationship memory: timed contacts, pinned notes, custom note types, photo attachments, and global timeline search.

Mode B

Safe Ledger

Streamlined, distraction-free address book protection with local-first storage and recovery backups.
