Privacy Policy — TidyContacts

Last updated: September 7, 2026
App: TidyContacts, bundle ID com.saeed.tidycontacts

Summary

Your contacts and notes are stored on your device, in the app's own database. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Two features send limited information off-device on your explicit action, described below. The app displays ads; the ad provider collects device identifiers for that purpose.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your contacts, notes, tags, and attachments, in the app's own local database — separate from, and in addition to, your phone's system address book.
  • Photos and files you attach to a contact's timeline.
  • Encrypted backups you create, until you export or delete them.

None of the above leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (e.g. using your phone's share sheet to send a backup file, or a contact's notes, to someone else).

Permissions this app requests, and why

PermissionWhy
Contacts (read and write)To display and organize your existing contacts, and to write changes (like deactivating a contact) back to your phone's address book.
CameraTo take a photo for a contact or a timeline attachment, and for on-device business-card scanning.
Photo libraryTo attach an existing photo to a contact or timeline entry.

No other permission is requested. This app does not request access to your call log, SMS messages, or precise location.

On-device processing

Business-card and text scanning (OCR) runs entirely on your device. No image or extracted text is sent anywhere for this feature.

What leaves your device, and why

Two features make network requests. Both are optional and only run when you use the specific feature.

1. Link preview / business enrichment. When you share a link (for example, a Google Maps link or a business website) into the app, it fetches basic public page information (name, address, photo) from that URL to help fill in a contact's details. The URL you share is contacted directly for this.

2. "Get business info" lookup. When you tap this button on a contact, the business name, address, and/or coordinates you're looking up are sent to our own server, which forwards the request to HERE Technologies' business-search service and returns the result. No other contact data is included in this request.

Neither feature sends your contacts, notes, or attachments anywhere. Both run only when you tap the specific button that triggers them.

Advertising

This app displays ads, provided by Google AdMob, to support the free version. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) to show and measure ads. Where required by law (including in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from the app's Settings screen. On iOS, you will also be asked for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency system.

You can remove ads with the one-time "remove ads" purchase, when available.

For more on how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-up to use the app.
  • We do not run analytics or crash-reporting that phones home, as of this policy's date.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not have server-side access to your contacts, notes, or attachments — the app's local database is the only copy, except for backups you choose to export yourself.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not designed to collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you delete it within the app. Deactivating a contact keeps it retrievable inside the app; permanently deleting it removes it. Uninstalling the app removes its local database unless you have exported a backup.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the date at the top.
