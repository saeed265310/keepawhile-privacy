Privacy Policy — TidyContacts

Last updated: September 13, 2026
App: TidyContacts (bundle ID com.saeed.tidycontacts)
Summary: Your contacts and notes are stored locally on your device in the app's own database. They are not sent to us or to any third party. Two features send limited information off-device upon your explicit action. The app also uses analytics and crash-reporting services, and displays ads; these providers collect device identifiers and bounded, non-personal usage data, never your contact content, as described below.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your contacts, notes, tags, category labels, and attachments in the app's own local database — separate from, and in addition to, your phone's system address book.
  • Photos and files you attach to a contact's timeline.
  • Encrypted backups you create, until you export or delete them.
  • A randomly generated per-installation identifier (UUID) stored locally in preferences, used solely to track your balance of business lookup credits.

None of the above leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it yourself (e.g. using your phone's share sheet to send a backup file, or a contact's notes, to someone else), or when using the business lookup / rewarded ad credit feature described below.

On-device processing

  • Business-card and text scanning (OCR): Runs entirely on your device using on-device machine learning. No image or extracted text is sent to any server.
  • Contact categorization & migration analysis: Heuristic classification, category suggestions (e.g., trades, services, hospitality), duplicate detection, and temporary duration calculations run 100% locally on your device. No contact names or details leave your device for analysis.

What leaves your device, and why

Two features make network requests. Both are optional and only run when you use the specific feature:

1. Link preview / business enrichment: When you share a link (for example, a Google Maps link or a business website) into the app, it fetches basic public page information (name, address, photo) from that URL to help fill in a contact's details. The URL you share is contacted directly for this.

2. "Get business info" lookup & lookup credits: When you tap this button on a contact:

  • The business name, address, and/or coordinates you are looking up are sent to our server, which forwards the request to HERE Technologies' business-search service and returns the result. No other contact data is included in this request.
  • The request includes the app's randomly generated per-installation identifier (X-Install-Id) so our server can track your remaining business lookup credits and prevent abuse.
  • When you watch an optional rewarded ad to earn additional business lookups, this installation identifier is passed as custom data to Google AdMob. Upon ad completion, Google's servers send a Server-Side Verification (SSV) callback directly to our server (POST /api/admob_ssv) confirming the ad was viewed, allowing our server to credit your installation.

Neither feature sends your contacts, notes, or attachments anywhere. Both run only when you tap the specific button that triggers them.

Analytics and crash reporting

This app uses two additional third-party services to understand how the app is used and to diagnose crashes:

  • Amplitude (product analytics). We track a small set of app events — such as completing first-run setup, importing contacts, deactivating or restoring a contact, and adding a timeline note — to understand which features are used. We do not send your contact names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, notes, attachments, or any other personal content to Amplitude. Only bounded, categorical information (for example, whether an attachment was included, or a bucketed count of contacts imported) is sent — never the underlying content or an identifier that maps back to a specific contact.
    • In the EEA and UK, Amplitude is off by default. You are asked to opt in with a one-time consent prompt the first time it would otherwise apply, and your answer can be changed at any time from the app's Settings screen.
    • Elsewhere, Amplitude is on by default and can be turned off at any time from the same Settings screen ("Share usage & diagnostic data").
    • Turning it off deletes any data already queued on your device waiting to be sent.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash and error diagnostics). If the app crashes or encounters certain internal errors (for example, during a backup, restore, or database update), a diagnostic report is sent to help us fix the problem. This is on for everyone, with no opt-out toggle, because it is a stability/quality service rather than behavioral tracking — it never contains your contacts, notes, or attachments, only technical information such as the app's version, device model, and the error itself.

Both services may receive standard device/app metadata (such as OS version, device model, and app version) as part of how they operate, independent of any content you enter into the app.

Advertising

This app displays ads, provided by Google AdMob, to support the free version. AdMob and its partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as your device's advertising ID) to show and measure ads. Where required by law (including in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice later from the app's Settings screen. On iOS, you will also be asked for tracking permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency system.

Additionally, when you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad to earn business lookup credits, the app sends the local installation identifier to Google as custom data for Server-Side Verification (SSV), so Google can notify our backend server directly to grant the credits.

You can remove banner and interstitial ads with the one-time "remove ads" purchase, when available.

For more on how Google uses this data, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or sign-up to use the app.
  • Our analytics service (Amplitude) never receives your contact names, notes, attachments, or any other personal content — see "Analytics and crash reporting," above.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not have server-side access to your contacts, notes, or attachments — the app's local database is the only copy, except for backups you choose to export yourself.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not designed to collect information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as the app is installed, or until you delete it within the app. Deactivating a contact keeps it retrievable inside the app; permanently deleting it removes it. Uninstalling the app removes its local database unless you have exported a backup.
