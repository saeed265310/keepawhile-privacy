How It Works

Contacts and notes that work in tandem

Standard address books clutter your phone with one-off numbers while forgetting every quote, loan, and serial plate photo. Keep Awhile connects the full lifecycle.

Pinned favourites on top, clean address book below, Timed tab one tap away.

Core Capabilities

Designed for real-world recall

Built for the everyday details that slip through a standard contacts list.

Timed Lifecycle

Temporary contacts that unclutter your phone

Give a contact a time limit when you save it. The time left shows on a clear badge, and the Timed tab lists everyone who is on the clock. Extend, keep, or move several contacts at once when a project wraps up.

Contextual Timeline

Remember everything about them

Every contact has a timeline of notes and attachments—what they quoted, what you lent them, or the photo of the boiler serial plate. Use five built-in note types or create your own across eight colours, and pin up to three notes to the top.

Your Rules on Expiry

Ask first by default, or auto-hide in Settings

When a contact's time is up, Keep Awhile nudges you to Keep, Extend, or Hide—or you can enable auto-hide in Settings. Hidden contacts leave your phone's address book while the full record stays safely inside the app, ready to restore in one tap.

Instant Recall

Find anything across every note

Forget the plumber's name two years later? Search covers names, phone numbers, note types, and the full text of your notes. The Timeline tab shows every note across every contact, newest first.

Local-First Architecture

Your data stays yours

No account required. Your address book and personal notes live on your phone, backed by recovery tools you control.

On your device

Your contacts, notes, custom types, and photo attachments are kept in the app's own local database on your phone—never uploaded to us.

Works offline

Adding, editing, searching, categorising, and restoring contacts never needs a network connection. Text recognition runs locally on your device.

Backups you control

Export a passphrase-encrypted database copy, or enable the optional plain-text vCard safety copy on your device that survives uninstalling the app.

Questions & Answers

Everything you need to know about how Keep Awhile handles your contacts.

Where is my data?

On your device. Your contacts, notes and attachments are not sent to us. The free version shows ads, and the ad and analytics services it uses collect device identifiers and limited usage data, never your contact content. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Managing contacts and notes needs no connection. Optional features such as business lookup use the network only when you tap them.

What happens when a contact expires?

By default, Keep Awhile asks you whether to keep, extend or hide the contact—or you can turn on auto-hide in Settings so expired contacts hide automatically. A hidden contact is removed from your phone's address book and kept in full inside the app, where Keep Awhile holds the only copy until you restore it.

Does it cost anything?

Free to start. Details at launch.

Can I get my contacts back?

Yes. Hidden contacts restore in one tap, the Recovery Center can undo recent changes, and you can export an encrypted backup or keep the plain-text vCard safety copy.
