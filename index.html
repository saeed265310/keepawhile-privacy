Keep them for a while. Then let them go.

A contacts app for the plumber, the hotel, the one-off electrician. Reachable now, out of the way later, never deleted.

Coming soon on Google Play Android 10 or later

Temporary contacts

Give a contact a time limit when you save it. The time left shows on a badge, and the Timed tab lists everyone who is on the clock. Extend, keep or move several at once.

Remember everything about them

Every contact has a timeline of notes and attachments. What they quoted, what you lent them, the photo of the serial plate. Pin up to three notes so the important ones stay on top.

Nothing leaves without you asking

When a contact's time is up, Keep Awhile asks. If you hide someone, they leave your phone's address book and the full record stays in the app. Restore them in one tap.

Find anything

Search covers names, numbers and the text of your notes. The Timeline tab shows every note across every contact, newest first.

Your data stays yours

On your device

Your contacts, notes and attachments are kept in the app's own database on your phone.

Works offline

Adding, editing, searching and restoring contacts never needs a connection.

Backups you control

Export an encrypted copy, or keep a plain-text vCard safety copy on your device.

Questions

Where is my data?

On your device. Your contacts, notes and attachments are not sent to us. The free version shows ads, and the ad and analytics services it uses collect device identifiers and limited usage data, never your contact content. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Managing contacts and notes needs no connection. Optional features such as business lookup use the network only when you tap them.

What happens when a contact expires?

Nothing happens on its own. Keep Awhile asks you whether to keep, extend or hide the contact. A hidden contact is removed from your phone's address book and kept in full inside the app, where Keep Awhile holds the only copy until you restore it.

Does it cost anything?

Free to start. Details at launch.

Can I get my contacts back?

Yes. Hidden contacts restore in one tap, the Recovery Center can undo recent changes, and you can export an encrypted backup or keep the plain-text vCard safety copy.
